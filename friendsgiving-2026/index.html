What is a "Board" Potluck?

Similar to a standard potluck, everyone brings a dish to share. The twist is that instead of a single dish, each guest brings a themed food board. Charcuterie boards are the classic choice, but get creative! Think a s'mores-gasboard, a harvest veggie board, or a leftovers board.

These do not have to be Thanksgiving themed! If you are running short on ideas, text Nick or Kelsey and we can help you brainstorm.

Drinks

We'll have a signature cocktail, hard seltzers, and bourbon to share. There will be some nonalcoholic beverages/soda as well, but otherwise bring what you'd like to drink.
